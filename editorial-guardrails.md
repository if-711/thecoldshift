# The Cold Shift — Editorial Guardrails

Internal document. Not published to the site. Enforced during content review.

## Prohibited Statements

The following statements must not appear in any public-facing content on The Cold Shift:

### Neuropause Protocol
1. Heart rate always spikes during Entry.
2. Breathing always accelerates.
3. Adrenaline is released at a specified point.
4. Control returns during Load.
5. Extended exhalation directly stimulates the vagus nerve.
6. The user shifts from sympathetic to parasympathetic activity.
7. The nervous system completes a full cycle.
8. Regulation or stabilization occurs by minute nine.
9. The physiological peak occurs between minutes six and nine.

### General Prohibited Claims
10. The Ice Sack regulates the nervous system.
11. The Ice Sack treats pain, inflammation, or medical conditions.
12. The Ice Sack activates the vagus nerve.
13. The Ice Sack improves mental health, reduces anxiety, or treats depression.
14. The Ice Sack boosts immunity.
15. The Ice Sack enhances athletic performance or accelerates recovery.
16. The Ice Sack burns fat or aids weight loss.
17. The Ice Sack is equivalent to meditation, breathwork, or therapy.
18. Short Ice Sack sessions affect skin sensation but not core temperature (unverified).

## Required Labels

Every BHVD conceptual model visible on the site must include:

- **Statement type:** BHVD conceptual model
- **Evidence confidence:** No direct evidence
- **Transfer beyond the session:** Untested

## Evidence Badge Rules

No evidence badge or classification label may appear in public-facing HTML unless:
1. It references a completed source record in the source ledger.
2. The claim has an Ice Sack applicability classification.
3. The claim has a limitations statement.
4. The claim has a last review date.
5. The claim has an approval status of "approved."

## Product Fact Rules

No product-specific specification, temperature claim, cooling profile, skin temperature effect,
core temperature effect, coverage area, pressure measurement, duration claim, or physiological
response claim may be published unless:
1. The wording maps to a verified specification or direct measurement.
2. The product fact record has a verification status of "verified."
3. The measurement method, measurement date, and measurer are documented.

## Delivery Method Integrity

Research from cold water immersion, cryotherapy, localized icing, environmental cold, or
unrelated cooling garments may be cited as **mechanistically relevant**. It is not **direct
evidence** for The Ice Sack.

Evidence confidence badges must not describe whether research transfers between delivery methods.

## Ownership Disclosure

The Cold Shift is a BHVD field project. BHVD owns and makes The Ice Sack.
This relationship must be disclosed in the footer, About page, and System page.

The Cold Shift is not a regulatory firewall. Product-linked educational claims receive the
same review standard as direct product marketing.
